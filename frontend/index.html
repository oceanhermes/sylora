Frequently asked

No surprises, we promise.

What's blockchain, in one sentence?

It's a shared ledger: every record is copied to thousands of computers at once, so nobody can quietly delete or change it. You don't deal with it — SYLORA does.

Do I need to pay to start?

No. Logging an action is free. There's only a small network fee (~$0.01) when your points move — like a postage stamp. The app shows you the exact cost before you sign.

What's MetaMask, and why do I need it?

MetaMask is a digital wallet on your phone or browser — like a bank account, but yours alone. It stores your points and proves "yes, this is my account." It holds SYL points, not real money.

Who decides if my action is approved?

Local organisers you trust — your bank-sampah, your youth group, your community. Their names are recorded on-chain; if they misbehave, they can be replaced. No single "evil admin".

Can the points be cashed out for real money?

No cash-out is available. SYLORA currently runs on BOT mainnet; redemptions use SYL points and record an event. Partner vouchers are planned.
